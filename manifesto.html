The Manifesto
of the Unimpressed.

To the founders, evangelists, venture priests, and professional future-havers who mistake attention for authority: your complimentary trial of unquestioned bullshit has expired.

CONFIDENCE IS NOT EVIDENCE
ARTICLE 01

Reality has declined your term sheet.

The universe does not care who led your round. Arithmetic does not take meetings. A theorem will not become true because you wore trainers on stage and said “at scale” with the solemnity of a bishop handling a relic.

You cannot buy a controlling interest in what happened. You can buy a microphone. The department regrets to inform you these are different assets.

ARTICLE 02

Determinism: the alibi detector.

Declare the inputs. Name the procedure. Record the environment. Define the result. If your supposedly deterministic process changes its answer, explain the changed condition. “The vibes evolved” is not a diff.

Randomness is allowed. Unannounced randomness dressed as certainty is not. Record the seed where applicable and disclose what the seed does not control. If your output depends on hardware, timing, hidden state, or a priest waving a GPU, stop pretending you specified the whole experiment.

A deterministic machine can be consistently wrong. Excellent: now we can reproduce exactly where your bullshit enters the pipeline. Reproducibility is the start of an investigation, not a certificate of divine innocence.

ARTICLE 03

Truth has no premium tier.

There is no enterprise subscription to being correct. A claim made in a spare room can survive a test. A claim announced from a billion-dollar stage can fail one. The test does not owe the stage an apology.

To the gatekeepers who demand an institution from a researcher but accept a personality from a founder: your standard is a guest list. Staple it to your forehead so the rest of us know which document we are dealing with.

ARTICLE 04

Provenance, or: who touched this shit?

Show the source. Show the transformations. Show who did what, when, with which version, under which assumptions. Retain the failed runs. Your favourite screenshot is not a chain of custody; it is a hostage photograph of one cooperative outcome.

A hash binds an exact set of bytes. It does not prove the bytes are honest, the premise is sound, or the author has ceased being a dickhead. A signature binds a claim to a key under stated verification assumptions. It does not make the claim true. Provenance makes the trail inspectable. Inspection still has a job.

“Trust me” asks us to lend you credibility. Provenance asks you to show your working. Your outrage at the second request has been retained as a supplementary exhibit.

ARTICLE 05

We demand the right to say “failed”.

A test that cannot reject your claim is a fan club. A benchmark selected after you saw the results is a victory parade looking for a war. A safety framework without a defined failure condition is a brochure impersonating a fire extinguisher.

Predeclare the criteria. Publish the limitations. Distinguish a demonstration from evidence, evidence from inference, and inference from the shit you made up in the taxi to the keynote.

When you fail, retain it. Fix it. Report it. Do not rebrand the failure as an emerging paradigm merely because “broken” does poorly in the investor newsletter.

ARTICLE 06

Your moat is a fucking curtain.

Some information legitimately needs protection. That does not entitle you to turn inaccessible evidence into a demand for unlimited public belief. State what can be checked, what cannot, and how much confidence the disclosed evidence actually earns.

“Proprietary” is not an answer to “does it work?” “Commercially sensitive” is not a measurement. “You wouldn’t understand” is an extraordinarily efficient way to tell us you cannot explain it.

We do not require every secret. We require you to stop charging public truth prices for private assertion inventory.

ARTICLE 07

Immutability is not amnesia.

Tag the release. Bind the evidence to the exact version. Keep the old record. If you correct it, publish the correction and its relationship to what came before.

Yesterday’s promise does not disappear because today’s website has a gradient. A silent edit is not learning in public; it is trying to leave the restaurant without paying for your previous sentence.

ARTICLE 08

The mirror is now a mandatory deliverable.

For every claim of inevitability, provide a condition under which you would admit you were wrong. For every claim of safety, provide a test capable of discovering danger. For every claim of openness, provide something more open than your calendar for sponsored interviews.

For every person you ignored while celebrating the same idea from someone with better access: stare into the mirror until “meritocracy” stops coming out of your mouth.

We are not here to worship the future you keep invoicing us for. We are here to inspect the present you keep failing to deliver.

FINAL ORDER / FORM GF-001

Respectfully lodged. Meticulously versioned. Reproducibly delivered.

Bring the logs. Bring the methods. Bring the failures. Bring one claim that can survive without your name attached to it.

Until then, your confidence is impounded, your aura is under review, and your licence to talk shit without receipts is REVOKED.
